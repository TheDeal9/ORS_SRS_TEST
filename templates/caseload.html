How these numbers are worked out
  • Each client counts once, using their first ORS (intake) and their latest ORS. Totals are out of 40.
  • Reliable change is a move of {{ rc }} points or more from intake; clinically significant adds crossing from below to above the client's cutoff. These are the same rules as the flags on each client page.
  • Effect size is the average change divided by the standard deviation of intake scores, for clients with two or more ORS. It is hidden below {{ min_n }} clients. Larger is better; use it to compare yourself over time, not to judge a single client.
  • These are descriptions of your caseload, not predictions, and not a comparison with other practitioners. Averages by session number exclude clients who have stopped.
  • The figures are calculated in the database from the saved scores, so they always match the client pages.
