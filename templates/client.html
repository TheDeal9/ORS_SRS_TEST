How to read these charts and flags
  • ORS is scored 0 to 40 (four lines of 0 to 10). Below the cutoff ({{ ors_cutoff }} here: 25 for adults, 28 for ages 13 to 17) is the clinical range, shaded red.
  • Reliable change is a move of 5 points or more from the first ORS (intake). The blue band shows the ±5 zone around intake: points outside it are reliable change. Some publishers use 6, so confirm for your training.
  • Clinically significant improvement is a reliable improvement that also crosses from below to above the cutoff. It is marked ▲ with a ring.
  • SRS is scored 0 to 40. A total below 36, or any single line below 9, is a prompt to invite the client to comment on the session; it is not a diagnosis. A ring marks sessions with a line below 9.
  • These charts describe results; they do not predict outcomes. Early-session change guidance is contested, so no "on track" verdict is shown.
  • Sources: NBCC, Miller and Duncan ORS/SRS cutoffs; CORC, ORS; Greenspace, ORS reliable change.
